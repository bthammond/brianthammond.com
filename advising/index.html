Advising · For Owner-Led & Family Businesses

One day this business changes hands. Right now it isn’t ready.

Succession isn’t a document — it’s a company that runs without you, and people prepared to take it. I help family and owner-led businesses build both, years before the pressure is on.

How this works

Three steps. You can stop at any of them.

01 · 30 minutes

The Call

You tell me what’s actually going on. I tell you whether I’m the right help — and if I’m not, who is. No deck, no discovery questionnaire, no proposal waiting at the end of it.

02 · 2 to 4 weeks

The Read

I look at the same four things every time: who is actually leading, how work moves, what the numbers say, and what happens to this business if you’re not in it. You get one page — what’s working, what it’s costing you, what to fix first. That page is yours either way.

03 · 90 days at a time

The Work

We fix the first thing. Then the next. You keep running the business; I keep the plan moving and hold people to it. We review at 90 days, and you can walk at any one of them.

What I promise you
  • Everything you tell me stays between us, including the parts about family.
  • I’ll tell you when you don’t need me.
  • You’ll never get a proposal you didn’t ask for.
  • I won’t sell you training you don’t need, even though I own a training company.

Where owners usually start

  • “I’m the bottleneck”
  • “My managers aren’t ready”
  • “I need to hand this off”
  • “I have nobody to ask”

Four situations, and they are almost always connected. You don’t have to know which one you’re in — that’s what the first call is for.

01 — When everything runs through you

Getting you out of the middle of your own company.

Processes that worked at 10 employees don’t work at 50. We find where work is actually stalling, put decisions in other people’s hands with limits they understand, and get your week back — before the ceiling costs you a year of growth.

02 — When your managers were never taught

Building the bench that carries this forward.

You can’t scale what only runs through you. Your best people got promoted for being good at the old job. We teach them the new one, so your team makes the call you would have made — and you hear about it at the weekly, not at 11pm.

03 — When the handoff is coming

Getting the business ready before you have to.

The best exits take years to build. Whether it goes to family, a partner, or the market, we get the business, the ownership structure and the people ready long before the pressure is on — so the decision stays yours.

04 — When you have nobody to ask

A confidential second opinion on the big calls.

Acquisitions, pivots, new markets, capital decisions — the choices that look simple on a spreadsheet and feel anything but. You get someone who has made these calls with his own money on the line, and who has no stake in which way you go.

Before you close this tab

What waiting costs.

Nobody loses the business by waiting. They lose the years.

  • Another eighteen months as the bottleneck — and eighteen months of growth you never see, because the ceiling is you.
  • The manager you were counting on leaves, because there was no path above them and nobody ever told them there would be.
  • The handoff gets decided for you — by a health event, by a buyer with a 30-day window, or by a family member who believed they were promised something in a conversation you don’t remember having.
  • And a company that only works when you’re in it sells at a discount, if it sells at all. Every year you stay in the middle of it, that discount grows.

None of this is urgent. That is exactly the problem.
